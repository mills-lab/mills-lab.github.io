A new paper from the SMaHT MEI Working Group, co-authored by Jinhao Wang, Weichen Zhou, and Ryan Mills, presenting a multi-platform framework for mapping somatic mobile element insertions in human tissues (combining long-read and MEI-targeted sequencing, haplotype phasing, and donor-specific genome assemblies), was published in <i>Cell Genomics</i>!
